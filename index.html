When you're stuck

Pick the smallest next action. You don't need to feel motivated first.

I'm overwhelmedWrite down everything. Circle one task that matters today. Ignore the rest for 15 minutes.
I can't get startedMake the first step ridiculously small: open the email, find the form, put the shoes by the door.
I'm distracted by someone else's problemAsk: does this need action today, and is it actually mine to solve?
I've fallen off trackDon't restart the whole plan. Do the next small thing. No guilt tax.
